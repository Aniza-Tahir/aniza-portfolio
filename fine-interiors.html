Brand & Digital Creative
Project Overview

Building a consistent visual direction for a growing interior design brand.

Fine Interiors is a Pakistani interior design and construction brand focused on home and office interiors, contracting, and building services. The project explored how a consistent visual identity could support the brand across digital and print touchpoints.

The creative work included social media posts, advertising creatives, business card design, typography, and brand kit development, with a focus on maintaining a cohesive and professional brand presence.
